Why the PET wood door

Built to last, soft to live with

A food-grade surface on a waterproof multilayer core — the look of timber, without the upkeep.

01

Food-grade PET finish

A safe, environmentally friendly surface that is stable, acid- and corrosion-resistant, soft to the touch and wipes clean.

02

Multilayer waterproof core

High-compression multilayer board that resists deformation and cracking, with superior waterproof performance.

03

Concealed soft hardware

Hidden hinges and a quiet, soft-close action, with a flush concealed frame for clean 90° reveals.

04

Finishes that match

Door colours and woodgrains chosen to sit seamlessly with your Luxova cabinetry and wardrobes.
