Step one

Door styles

Eight door profiles — from the universally loved Shaker to handleless slab and framed glass.

Shaker

Recessed five-piece panel

The timeless one. A clean recessed center panel in a square frame — at home in any kitchen, traditional to transitional.

Slim Shaker

Narrow frame, tight reveal

A slimmer rail and crisper edge for a quieter, more contemporary take on the Shaker look.

Double Shaker

Double-frame profile

An extra inner frame adds depth and a tailored, furniture-grade feel that reads custom.

Classic

Raised center panel

A softly raised panel with traditional warmth — the choice for a richer, more formal kitchen.

Charm

Natural woodgrain front

Textured woodgrain fronts that bring organic character and a relaxed, lived-in mood.

Slab

Flat handleless front

A seamless flat front with no frame — the clean, modern, handleless look.

Glass Front

Framed glass insert

A glass-panel door to show off dishware or add light — mixes into any run.

Beadboard

Grooved cottage panel

A vertically grooved center panel for a relaxed farmhouse or coastal feel.
